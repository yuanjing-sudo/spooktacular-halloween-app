How to Wander

Move

WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump · Ctrl crouch

F toggle flight · Space/C rise & descend · V third person

Touch: left stick to walk, drag the right side to look.

Forage

E interact with carrots, rabbits, campfires and fallen wood.

1 eat a carrot · 2 plant a seed · Q call bonded rabbits.

Ripe carrots have a full leaf fan. Water a sprout to double its growth.

Campfires

Stand close and press E for the fire menu: light it, add wood, cook carrots or fish, roast marshmallows, warm up or sit for a story.

Rain can douse a fire. Fuel burns down. Cold drains warmth; warmth drains health.

Fish & pumpkins

Face a misty pond until Cast Line appears, then E to cast. Strike when the bobber dips, then hold E to reel and release when the tension bar reddens.

Wild pumpkins can be carved into glowing jack-o’-lanterns. Press 6 to eat a fish.

Wildlife

Owls perch in the pines after dark. Stand beneath one and listen; they trade forest whispers for your attention.

Bats circle at dusk, frogs croak from the ponds, hedgehogs curl when you come close, and moths spiral around lit fires.

The Lantern Road

Small quests appear in the tracker on the left. They teach the forest's systems and reward wood, seeds, fish and stranger things.

Open the journal's Quests tab to see the whole road.

Rabbits

Feed carrots or pet gently to earn trust. At 72% a rabbit bonds and follows you.

At night, bonded rabbits and wild ones gather near lit fires. Kits stay close to the warren.

The world

Chunks stream in 16-metre squares around you. X and Z never end. Floating spirit isles hide crystals above the canopy.

Five biomes: Whispering Pines, Pumpkin Hollow, Misty Meadow, Ashen Grove and Frostveil.

Keep a record

J journal · M map & stats · P photo mode · F3 debug.

Photos download as PNG. The forest autosaves; you can also save from the pause menu.
